5|HirePathAI brings job searching, resume review and skill development into one web portal. It is intended for students, recent graduates and other job seekers who need help relating their qualifications to the requirements of a particular role. Finding a vacancy is only one part of this process: candidates also need to understand the skills an employer expects and present their experience clearly. The project addresses these needs through job search and application facilities, resume analysis, skill-gap identification, learning links and resume-building support. Its aim is to help candidates make informed choices about suitable opportunities and the preparation those opportunities require.
6|By connecting these services, HirePathAI gives candidates a clearer path from reviewing a job description to improving their resumes and preparing an application. Skill comparisons highlight areas to investigate, while learning resources and resume-building support help users act on the results. The project therefore combines employment search with practical career preparation.
