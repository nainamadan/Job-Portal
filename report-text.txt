                                     ABSTRACT

HirePathAI is an AI-powered job portal designed to make the job search and recruitment
process easier, faster, and more efficient for students, freshers, and job seekers. In today's
competitive world, finding a suitable job according to educational qualifications, skills,
experience, and career interests is a major challenge. Many candidates are unable to identify the
skills required for their desired jobs or prepare a professional resume. HirePathAI aims to solve
these problems by providing a smart and user-friendly platform that connects job seekers with
suitable career opportunities.
The main objective of this project is to help candidates find relevant jobs, analyze their resumes,
identify missing skills, and improve their professional profiles. The system provides various
features to support users throughout their job search journey.

Key Features of HirePathAI:
    1. User Registration and Login: Allows users to create an account, log in, and manage
         their personal profiles.
    2. Job Search and Apply: Helps users explore available job opportunities and apply for
         jobs according to their interests and qualifications.
    3. Resume Analysis: Analyzes uploaded resumes and checks the skills and information
         mentioned in them.
    4. Missing Skill Detection: Identifies the skills that are missing from a candidate's profile
         according to the selected job requirements.
    5. YouTube Learning Links: Provides useful YouTube learning resources to help
         candidates learn missing skills and improve their knowledge.
    6. Resume Builder: Helps users create or improve their resumes in a professional format.

HirePathAI aims to reduce the time and effort required for job searching by providing multiple
career-related services on a single platform. It helps candidates understand their skill gaps,
improve their resumes, develop new skills, and find suitable employment opportunities.
Overall, HirePathAI provides a smart and organized approach to job searching and career
development by using Artificial Intelligence to improve the job-seeking experience and help
candidates move towards better career opportunities.

1.1 Topic of the System
Project Title: HirePathAI ­ AI-Powered Job Portal
HirePathAI is a web-based job portal developed to help students, freshers, and job seekers
find suitable employment opportunities. The idea behind this project is to make the job
searching process easier by bringing different career-related facilities together on one
platform.
Finding a suitable job is not always easy, especially for candidates who are starting their
careers. Many students complete their education but are not fully aware of the technical
skills required by companies. Some candidates also face problems while preparing their
resumes or selecting jobs according to their abilities.
HirePathAI provides a platform where users can explore job opportunities, select their
desired job roles, upload resumes, and check whether their skills match the selected job
requirements. It also helps candidates find the skills they need to improve and provides
YouTube learning links for further learning.
The system focuses on connecting a candidate's existing skills with the requirements of
available jobs. Its main purpose is to help users make better career decisions and prepare
themselves for employment opportunities.

1.2 Project Overview
HirePathAI is designed as an online platform that supports candidates during different
stages of their job search. It provides facilities for account creation, job exploration, resume
checking, skill improvement, and job applications.
A user first registers or logs in to the portal. After entering the platform, the user can
explore different companies and available job positions. Once a suitable job is selected, the

The resume analysis facility examines the skills present in the uploaded resume and
compares them with the requirements of the selected job. If some required skills are not
found, the system identifies them as missing skills. This gives candidates a clearer idea of
what they should learn before applying for a particular position.
The portal also provides YouTube learning links related to missing skills. Users can follow
these resources to improve their technical knowledge. In addition, the resume builder
facility helps candidates prepare or improve their resumes.
The main facilities provided by HirePathAI are:
1. Registration and Login: Users can create accounts and access the job portal through

    their login details.
2. Job Search: Candidates can explore available jobs and select positions according to

4. Resume Analysis: The system checks the uploaded resume and identifies the skills

5. Missing Skill Detection: It finds the skills that are required for a selected job but are

6. YouTube Learning Links: It provides learning resources that help users develop the

    skills they are missing.
7. Job Matching: The system helps candidates understand the suitability of their profiles

    for selected jobs through matching scores and recommendations.
8. Resume Builder: Users can create or improve their resumes to present their

    qualifications and skills in a better way.
By combining these facilities, HirePathAI aims to make the job search process more
organized and useful for candidates.

2.1 Introduction to Job Portals
A job portal is an online platform that helps people search for employment opportunities
and allows companies to share information about vacant positions. It provides a common
place where job seekers can explore different career options without visiting companies
individually.
In earlier times, people mainly depended on newspapers, personal contacts, advertisements,
and direct visits to companies to find jobs. With the growth of internet technology, the
process of searching and applying for jobs has become more convenient. Candidates can
now explore job openings, read job descriptions, and submit their resumes through online
platforms.
Job portals are useful for both candidates and employers. Candidates can search for jobs
according to their qualifications, experience, and interests, while employers can publish
vacancies and receive applications from interested people.
The common facilities available on job portals include:
1. User Registration: Allows candidates to create their accounts and provide personal

    information.
2. Job Search: Helps users find available vacancies according to different job roles and

    requirements.
3. Resume Upload: Allows candidates to upload their resumes for job applications.
4. Job Application: Provides an option to apply for available positions online.
5. Company Information: Displays details about companies and their available

    opportunities.
6. Job Notifications: Helps users stay informed about new job openings.
7. Profile Management: Allows candidates to update their educational details, skills, and

    other information.
HirePathAI is developed with the same basic purpose of providing job-related services.
However, it also focuses on resume analysis and missing skill identification to help
candidates understand their preparation level for a selected job.

2.3 Role of Artificial Intelligence in Recruitment
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables computer systems to perform tasks
that normally require human intelligence, such as understanding information, identifying

System implementation is an important phase of software development in which the designed
system is converted into a working software application. It involves developing different
modules, integrating their functionalities, connecting the database, and providing an interface
through which users can interact with the system.
The implementation of the HirePathAI Job Portal focuses on developing an intelligent platform
that helps candidates search for suitable employment opportunities and improve their
professional profiles.
The system provides different facilities, including user registration, login, profile management,
job searching, resume uploading, resume analysis, missing-skill detection, job
recommendations, learning resources, and resume building.
The application follows a structured approach in which users first create an account or log into
the existing account. After successful authentication, users can select their preferred company or
job position and upload their resumes.
The system analyses the uploaded resume and compares the available skills with the
requirements of the selected job. Based on this comparison, the system identifies missing skills
and provides relevant learning resources.
The platform also provides job recommendations and matching information to help candidates
understand how closely their profiles match available job opportunities.
The main objective of implementation is to develop a functional and user-friendly job portal
that reduces the effort required for job searching and supports candidates in improving their
career opportunities.
Objectives of System Implementation
The main objectives of implementing HirePathAI are:

    1. To provide a simple and user-friendly job portal.
    2. To allow users to register and log into the application.
    3. To maintain candidate profile information.
    4. To provide job searching and application facilities.
    5. To allow candidates to upload their resumes.
    6. To analyse resumes according to job requirements.
    7. To identify missing skills in candidate profiles.
    8. To recommend suitable job opportunities.

5.6 Missing Skill Detection
Missing Skill Detection is an important feature of HirePathAI. It helps candidates identify the
skills that are required for a selected job but are not found in their resumes.
The module compares the candidate's available skills with the required skills of the selected job
position.
The purpose of this process is to help candidates understand their skill gaps and improve their
professional knowledge.
For example, a Frontend Developer position may require HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and
Bootstrap.
If the candidate's resume contains HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap, but React is not
identified, the system can display React as a missing skill.
Working of Missing Skill Detection
The process follows these steps:

    1. The candidate selects a job position.
    2. The system obtains the required skills.
    3. The candidate uploads a resume.
    4. The system identifies the skills available in the resume.
    5. The system compares both skill lists.
    6. The system identifies the missing skills.
    7. The results are displayed to the candidate.

5.7.2 Matching Score
A matching score represents the similarity between the candidate's available skills and the
required skills of a selected job.
A basic skill-based matching score can be calculated using the following formula:
Matching Score = (Matched Skills / Total Required Skills) × 100
For example:
Total required skills = 5
Matched skills = 4
Matching Score = (4 / 5) × 100
Matching Score = 80%
This means that 4 out of the 5 required skills were identified in the candidate's profile.
The score is an illustrative example. A complete recommendation system may also consider
education, experience, and other factors.
